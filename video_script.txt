SECTION 4 — THE CHALLENGE                          [2:30–3:05]
--------------------------------------------------------------
[VISUAL: Back to face on camera]

"The hardest part of building this wasn't the code — it was
when the engine kept getting the answer wrong.

Every single run, it pointed to the crash site itself as the
root cause. I spent a while staring at that before I figured
out what was happening.

The scoring was set up to reward proximity — functions closer
to the crash score higher. Which sounds right, until you
realize that at zero hops, proximity always wins. A real
culprit hiding three function calls downstream could never
outscore the crash site, no matter what other signals said.

I rebalanced the weights — made git recency the top signal
instead of proximity — and made the search go in both
directions. The moment it correctly traced to that config
bug three hops away, I knew it actually worked."
